Readings
  - B-orig reproduces the flip on both Linux legs: write_string_bytes out of line (x86 1278 + 837
    cold, arm64 2012), write() 17501 -> 16640 (x86) / 27100 -> 23512 (arm64).
  - bfix-v1 brought write_string_bytes back inline and write_mapping_uncached to main's bytes, but
    Frame::Frame kept B's 417 + 39 cold (main 260) and was no longer inlined into write_mapping
    (838, main 1194) or one site of write() (x86 calls: 2 Frame::Frame calls, main 1): the three
    framed native writers were a second flip of the same kind. NativeFrame removes it.
  - bfix: write, write_mapping_body and write_mapping call exactly what main's call (both ISAs);
    no write_string / write_string_bytes symbol; Frame::Frame 260 (x86) / 344 (arm64), main's.
    arm64 (no machine-function splitting): write_mapping_body 8500, write_mapping 1676,
    write_mapping_uncached 3740 -- main's bytes and opcodes; write() 27160 vs 27100, +15
    instructions, which are the V4 tail (flag load, test, branches, the tail call to write_native
    and its own epilogue: ldp x7, mov x2 -- static check 1's +5/+7).
    x86: write() 17803 + 10917 cold vs 17501 + 11172 (+16 instructions: the same tail, cmpb/je/jmp,
    popq x6 epilogue); write_mapping_body 6182 + 3116 vs 6091 + 3217 (opcodes: -2 jmp, -1 nop);
    write_mapping 1266 + 432 vs 1194 + 482 (+1 nopl); write_mapping_uncached identical. The x86
    residuals outside the tail are hot/cold split placement only. The per-function block counts of
    these writers are identical in profile-A and profile-B; the profile summary is not (B: 511
    functions, 6319 blocks, total count 589342900; A: 454, 5183, 587046224), and
    -fsplit-machine-functions reads its cold threshold from that summary.
